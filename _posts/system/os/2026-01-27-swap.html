  • kswapd
    • periodic/direct reclaim the memory.
  • swappiness
    • Lower values decrease the amount of swap. A value of 0 instructs the kernel not to initiate swap until the amount of free and file-backed pages is less than the high water mark in a zone.
  • priority
    • useful when multi swap files or partitions.
